Our mission

Many children in rural and underserved communities have the curiosity and ability to learn but little access to books, technology or guidance. At the same time, many people want to help but do not know which causes need support or how to find them. KindMind AI is an attempt to close that gap with clear information, honest funding goals and an AI assistant that explains things simply.

KindMind AI is inspired by the vision of Mujeeba Foundation for Learning and Development, an organization that is still being developed and is not yet registered.

How it works

  1. Discover. Browse educational causes and read what each one needs and how funds are intended to be used.
  2. Ask. Use the AI assistant to ask questions in your own words. It answers only from the project information on the site.
  3. Support. Choose an amount and complete a PayPal Sandbox test payment to see how giving would work.

Status of this demo: the pages are live. The AI assistant and the PayPal Sandbox checkout are being connected and will be marked as working only after they are tested.

Transparency and responsible giving

Clearly labelled demo data

Every project here is sample content. No beneficiaries, statistics or success stories are claimed.

Test payments only

Payments run through the PayPal Sandbox. They are simulations, and no real money is collected.

AI gives information, not verification

The assistant explains project information. It does not verify organizations and can make mistakes.

Honest about what is unknown

If information is not available, the assistant is designed to say so instead of guessing.
